ED50 is a limited company that needed to secure its clients’ data, manage its invoices, and separate personal data from company data. That’s where this homelab came from: a cheap, secure infrastructure that was quick to deploy. After a few months with the services running, while looking at job postings, I realized this project wasn’t enough to show what I can really do: they were asking for Terraform, Kubernetes, Python...

I asked myself what the best way was to show a recruiter that I’m capable. DevOps/SRE isn’t an entry-level role, and that has me competing against people with far more experience than me.

The answer I found was to apply the practices of a real enterprise infrastructure: migrating to IaC, using Kubernetes, building a real delivery pipeline... All of it will run on a single Proxmox host. A hardware failure would leave the system down until I rebuild it, and that’s a risk I accept because the purpose of this project is to practice these skills. What I can’t accept is losing data, and that’s already covered: the backups follow the 3-2-1 strategy and have passed restore tests.

Over time, I’ll build up hours maintaining and updating a system. Even though the company has a single user (the CEO), this project will show that I can build an enterprise infrastructure and maintain it over time.
