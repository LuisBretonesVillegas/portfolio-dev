Looking at the ED50 v2.0 project, I've decided to prepare for the Terraform (TA-004) and Kubernetes (CKA) certifications. In this project I've limited my use of AI, because what I'm after is testing my own knowledge, not being efficient. If I'm not going to lean on AI, I need to learn these technologies in a structured way, and a certification gives me a syllabus and a way to measure my progress.

The order I plan to take them in is Terraform -> CCNA -> Kubernetes. I've moved Terraform forward because I know enough networking to take on the project, but I don't know anything about IaC. This doesn't change the CCNA date: I'm still aiming for February 2028 to make use of the UAL discount.

In the meantime, I'll keep preparing for the CCNA in parallel, with at least one day a week on Jeremy's IT Lab course.

The migration to IaC will start once I finish Bryan Krausen's "HashiCorp Certified Terraform Associate 004 Exam Prep Course", and it will let me review the exam content in practice. Kubernetes will come into the more advanced phases of the project.

This order trades speed for depth. I'm starting with Terraform because it's the skill ED50 v2.0 needs right now, the CCNA stays on track for February 2028, and Kubernetes will arrive once the project is far enough along to need it. I'll write up what I learn at each stage here, including whatever turns out harder than I expected.
